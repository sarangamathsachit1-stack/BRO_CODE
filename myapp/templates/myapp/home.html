About ZORO

At Zoro, we believe that financial transparency isn't just about spreadsheets—it’s about empowering teams to collaborate without friction. Budgeting in large organizations is often a battlefield of conflicting priorities and siloed communication. We’ve changed that.

Zoro is a state-of-the-art Real-Time Collaborative Budget Allocator designed for high-stakes environments like IT firms, healthcare systems, educational institutions, and financial houses. We provide a single source of truth where fiscal planning meets real-time execution.

Our Mission

To eliminate the "budgeting vacuum" by providing institutions with an intelligent, automated ecosystem where every dollar is tracked, every conflict is arbitrated, and every stakeholder is aligned.

What Sets Zoro Apart?

Intelligent Arbitration: Gone are the days of endless meetings to resolve funding overlaps. Zoro’s proprietary conflict engine uses rule-based priority logic to resolve competing requests instantly. Cascading Accuracy: Our system is alive. A single adjustment in one department triggers an immediate, live recalculation across the entire organization, ensuring you never over-allocate.

Total Hierarchy Integration: From an entry-level employee raising a fund request to a Department Manager and ultimately the Finance Head or CEO, Zoro creates a seamless chain of command. Immutable Integrity: With a full audit trail and "point-in-time" rollback capabilities, we ensure that your financial history is transparent, accountable, and secure.

Tailored for Growth

Whether you are a multi-specialty hospital managing life-saving equipment funds, a university balancing departmental grants, or a local bank optimizing operational costs, Zoro scales with you. Our subscription-based model allows you to define your departments, manage your workforce, and monitor your entire fiscal ecosystem from a single, intuitive dashboard.
